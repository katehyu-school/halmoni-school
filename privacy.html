Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026  ·  doranchae.com

Doranchae is a small, family-run online Korean language school. We never ask for your real name, your email address, or anything else that identifies you — an account is a name you invent and a PIN. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties.

1. Who We Are, and What This Covers

Doranchae is an online Korean language school run by Kate HaeOk Shin Yu — one Korean teacher, not a company. You can reach us at hello@doranchae.com.

This policy covers doranchae.com and the Doranchae app — everything offered on this site. A separate class is taught privately to children in the teacher's own family. It is closed to anyone outside the family, there is no way to join it, and it is not covered by this policy.

2. What Information We Collect

  • A name you choose and a PIN — this is the whole of an account. The name can be anything you like; we never ask for your real name and have no way of knowing it. PINs are verified server-side and are never exposed to the browser.
  • Learning progress — completed episodes, quiz and test scores, and vocabulary review status. This is stored in your browser (localStorage). For enrolled students it is also copied to our server, so the teacher can see how lessons are going and so a record survives a lost or cleared browser. Visitors without an account, and trial accounts, are never copied to the server.
  • Notes and writing — anything you type in My Notes, including guided-writing exercises, stays in your browser only. It is never sent to our server.

We do not collect your email address, home address, phone number, real name, or payment information through the app. Because of this we cannot tell who you are, and we cannot recover a forgotten account name or PIN for you.

The speaking exercises use your microphone so you can hear yourself, but the recording stays in your browser and is discarded once you have played it back — it is never uploaded. The app never uses your camera.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To let you log in and access your class materials.
  • To let the teacher see how enrolled students are progressing, and to improve the lessons where learners get stuck.
  • To personalize your in-class experience (e.g., displaying your name on the welcome screen).

We do not use your information for advertising, automated profiling, or any purpose beyond running the class.

4. How We Store Your Information

Account data (the name you chose and your PIN) is stored in Supabase, a cloud database service with encryption at rest and in transit. Access is protected by Row-Level Security policies — anonymous visitors cannot read PINs or sensitive fields.

Learning progress is kept in your browser's localStorage. For enrolled students a copy is also stored in Supabase alongside the account it belongs to, under the same Row-Level Security protection — it can only be read by the teacher. Notes and writing are never copied; they remain in your browser.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — database and authentication backend.
  • Typecast (typecast.ai) — text-to-speech audio generation for lesson content.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — fonts loaded on page render.
  • GitHub Pages (github.com) — web hosting for doranchae.com.

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

6. Children

Doranchae is for ages 13 and up, and children under 13 cannot sign up for it. In any case an account holds only a name you invent and a PIN, and we have no way to tell how old you are. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account without a parent's knowledge, write to us and we will remove it.

If a younger child would like to learn here, a parent or guardian can give consent using the parental consent form, and we will create the account by hand. That form explains exactly what is kept for that child.

7. Your Rights

Because we hold nothing that identifies you, most of this you can do yourself, without asking us:

  • Access — everything we hold for a public account is what you can already see: the name you chose, your PIN, and your study progress.
  • Correction — you can change your PIN yourself from the home page (🔑 Change my PIN).
  • Deletion — an account you created yourself is deleted automatically after 30 days without a sign-in, and its progress record goes with it. Just stop using it. To clear what is on your device, clear your browser data for this site. (Accounts set up by the teacher are kept until the teacher removes them.)

We cannot act on a request about a specific account, because we have no way to confirm it is yours — the same reason we cannot recover a lost PIN. Where a student was enrolled by a teacher who knows them personally, requests are handled directly with that student or their parent; see section 6.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and post a notice on the message board inside the app.
