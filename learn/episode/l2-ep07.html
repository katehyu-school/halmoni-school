Level 2초급 · Elementary🍽️ 음식·식당 · Food & Dining

I can make invitations and offer to do things for others.

등장인물 — 라온 · 할머니

이야기 맛보기

  • 라온할머니, 점심 먹으려고 하는데 같이 가실래요?Grandma, I'm about to go get lunch — would you like to come with me?
  • 라온제가 사 드릴게요.I'll buy it for you.
  • 할머니뭐 먹을 거야?What are we going to eat?
  • 라온시원한 냉면 어때요?How about some refreshing naengmyeon?
  • 할머니나는 찬 음식은 먹지 못해.I can't eat cold food.
