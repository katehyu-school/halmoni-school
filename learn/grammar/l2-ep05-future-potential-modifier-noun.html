-(으)ㄹ + 명사Future/Potential Modifier + Noun

Level 2핵심 문법🏯 문화·역사 · Culture & History
Verb stem + -ㄹ (vowel/ㄹ ending) / -을 (consonant ending) + Noun

The third and final 관형절 form — completing the trilogy! While -(으)ㄴ describes completed actions and -는 describes ongoing ones, -(으)ㄹ describes future, intended, or potential actions. Think: 'the person who WILL go / something I'm GOING TO do.' See L2 ep01 for the full -(으)ㄴ/는/ㄹ comparison table.

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

같이 갈 사람?
Anyone who wants to come along? (lit. 'a person who will go together')

규칙

가다 → 갈 사람 · 오다 → 올 사람 · 살다 → 살 곳
먹다 → 먹을 것 · 읽다 → 읽을 책 · 찾다 → 찾을 길
-(으)ㄴ (past/adj) → 간 사람 · -는 (present) → 가는 사람 · -(으)ㄹ (future) → 갈 사람

예문

Vowel / ㄹ stems → -ㄹ

  • 같이 갈 사람a person who will go together
  • 마실 물water to drink
  • 볼 영화a movie to watch

Consonant stems → -을

  • 먹을 것something to eat
  • 읽을 책a book to read
  • 찾을 장소a place to find/look for
