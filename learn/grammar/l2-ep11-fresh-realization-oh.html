-네요Fresh realization — 'Oh, it is!' / 'How ___!'

Level 2미리보기🌱 환경·변화 · Environment & Change
Verb/Adj stem + -네요 · N이네요 / 네요

Use -네요 when you just noticed something or realized something for the first time in that moment. It expresses mild surprise or a new impression.

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

안타깝네요.
That's such a shame (I just feel it now).

규칙

Fresh impression · 즉각적인 반응Said in the moment of noticing or feeling something:
경치가 정말 아름답네요! (The view is really beautiful!)
생각보다 맵네요. (It's spicier than I thought.)
오늘 날씨 좋네요. (Oh, the weather's nice today.)
Tone range · 뉘앙스Mild surprise or fresh realization — NOT strong emotion
✅ 오늘 날씨가 좋네요. (Oh, the weather's nice today.)
✅ 한국어를 잘 하시네요! (Oh, your Korean is great!)
❌ 너무 무서워요! (This is scary!) → 무섭네요 would be calmer
-네요 vs -아/어요 · 차이-아/어요: plain fact statement
날씨가 좋아요. (The weather is good.)
-네요: fresh realization, you just noticed
날씨가 좋네요. (Oh, the weather is nice!) ← just stepped outside

예문

  • 한국어 잘 하네요!Oh, you're good at Korean!
  • 이 음식 맛있네요.This food is really good!
  • 경치가 정말 아름답네요!Oh wow, the scenery is truly beautiful!
