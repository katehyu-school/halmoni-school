-지만But / However

Level 2핵심 문법🌤️ 날씨·여가 · Weather & Leisure
Verb/adj stem + -지만 + (contrasting clause)

-지만 connects two contrasting ideas — like 'but' or 'however'. The easiest contrast connector in Korean: just attach -지만 to any verb or adjective stem, no vowel rule needed!

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

옷은 젖었지만 더 재미있어!
My clothes got wet, but it's even more fun!

규칙

-지만 = but / however (no vowel rule! ✨)오다 → 오지만 · 먹다 → 먹지만
좋다 → 좋지만 · 피곤하다 → 피곤하지만
Same for ALL verbs and adjectives!
Pattern: [fact] + -지만, [contrast]옷은 젖었지만 더 재미있어! Clothes got wet, but it's even more fun!
비싸지만 맛있어요. It's expensive, but it's delicious.
💡 -지만 vs -아/어도 — what's the difference?비가 오지만 가요. It's raining, but I'll go. (simple contrast)
비가 와도 가요. Even if it rains, I'll still go. (emphasis on 'regardless')

형태별 정리

형태쓰임예
-지만모든 어간에 동일하게 붙음 (받침 무관)크다 → 크지만, 먹다 → 먹지만, 좋다 → 좋지만

예문

  • 옷은 젖었지만 더 재미있어!My clothes got wet, but it's even more fun!
  • 작지만 아주 맛있어요.It's small, but very delicious.
  • 비싸지만 사고 싶어요.It's expensive, but I want to buy it.
  • 피곤하지만 계속 할게요.I'm tired, but I'll keep going.
💡 팁앞 문장과 뒷 문장이 반대되는 내용일 때 써요. 영어의 'but / however'에 해당해요. · Connects two contrasting ideas — simple and reliable!
