-아/어야겠다Resolve — 'I should…' / 'I'd better…'

Level 2미리보기🌱 환경·변화 · Environment & Change
Verb stem (ㅏ/ㅗ) + -아야겠다 · Verb stem (other) + -어야겠다

Use this when you've just decided you need to do something — a personal resolution in the moment.

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

그럼 여기서 맛있는 점심을 먹고 가야겠어요!
Then we should definitely have a delicious lunch here before we go!

규칙

Personal resolve · 결심In-the-moment decision to do something:
일찍 자야겠어요. (I should sleep early.)
좀 더 열심히 공부해야겠다. (I'd better study harder.)
이제 가야겠어요. (I think it's time to go.)
-아/어야겠다 vs -아/어야 해요 · 차이-아/어야겠다 = self-directed resolve (I've decided I should)
일찍 자야겠어요. (I should sleep early — my own decision)
-아/어야 해요 = external obligation or rule
8시까지 와야 해요. (You have to be here by 8 — it's required)
Trigger context · 언제 써요?You realize something and make an in-the-moment decision:
비가 오네. 우산 챙겨야겠다. (Oh it's raining. I should grab an umbrella.)
배가 고프네. 뭔가 먹어야겠다. (I'm hungry. I should eat something.)

예문

  • 운동을 더 해야겠어.I should exercise more.
  • 이 책 다시 읽어야겠어요.I should read this book again.
  • 이 단어 꼭 외워야겠어요.I really should memorize this word.
