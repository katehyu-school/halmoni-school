_(으)ㄹ 거예요 — 미래형 (Future Tense)will / going to · talking about plans

Level 1핵심 문법🏯 문화·역사 · Culture & History

동사 어간 + (으)ㄹ 거예요

To say you WILL do something or are GOING TO do something, attach -(으)ㄹ 거예요 to the verb stem. This is the main way to express future plans in Korean!

규칙

먹다 → 먹 + 을 거예요 → 먹을 거예요
가다 → 가 + ㄹ 거예요 → 갈 거예요
_(으)ㄹ 겁니다 is more formal · often seen in writing

예문

  • 경복궁도 방문할 거예요.We're going to visit Gyeongbokgung too.
  • 내일 친구를 만날 거예요.I will meet a friend tomorrow.
  • 뭐 먹을 거예요?What are you going to eat?
