V-(으)ㄹ까? — Shall we? / I wonder?Making a suggestion or thinking aloud

Level 1핵심 문법🚌 교통·길찾기 · Getting Around
동사 어간 + (으)ㄹ까?

제안하거나 혼자 생각할 때 · Use to suggest something or wonder aloud

-(으)ㄹ까? has two uses: (1) suggesting to someone — '갈까? = Shall we go?' (2) wondering to yourself — '뭐 먹을까? = I wonder what to eat?' Use 을까 after a consonant stem, ㄹ까 after a vowel stem.

규칙

💡 -(으)러 + -(으)ㄹ까? 세트로! · They go great together!
먹으러 갈까? = Shall we go eat?
보러 갈까? = Shall we go see (it)?
사러 갈까? = Shall we go buy (something)?
