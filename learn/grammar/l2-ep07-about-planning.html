-(으)려고 하다About to / Planning to

Level 2핵심 문법🍽️ 음식·식당 · Food & Dining
Verb stem + -(으)려고 하다 · Vowel/ㄹ stem → -려고 하다 · Consonant stem → -으려고 하다

Use -(으)려고 하다 when you're just about to do something or have a plan in mind right now. It's like saying 'I'm going to...' or 'I'm thinking of...' — the action is imminent or intended. Stronger than just -(으)ㄹ 거예요 because it implies you've already made up your mind.

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

점심 먹으려고 하는데 같이 가실래요?
I'm about to go get lunch — would you like to come?

규칙

가다 → 가려고 해요 · 오다 → 오려고 해요
보다 → 보려고 해요 · 만들다 → 만들려고 해요
먹다 → 먹으려고 해요 · 읽다 → 읽으려고 해요
찾다 → 찾으려고 해요 · 앉다 → 앉으려고 해요
하는데 at the end makes it a softer setup for a request:
점심 먹으려고 하는데 같이 가실래요?
= I'm (thinking of) getting lunch — (so) would you like to come?
-는데 = 'and.../ by the way...' — sets up context for what follows.
-(으)ㄹ 거예요 = future prediction or plan (less certain)
-(으)려고 하다 = intention, already decided in your mind
내일 갈 거예요 = I'll probably go tomorrow.
내일 가려고 해요 = I'm planning to go tomorrow (I've already decided).

예문

Vowel stem

  • 뭐 사려고 해요?What are you planning to buy?
  • 친구를 만나려고 해요.I'm planning to meet a friend.

Consonant stem

  • 점심 먹으려고 해요.I'm about to have lunch.
  • 한국어를 배우려고 해요.I'm planning to learn Korean.
