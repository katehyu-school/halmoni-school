보다 비교Comparison: more than ~

Level 2핵심 문법🛒 쇼핑·가입 · Shopping & Membership
A보다 (훨씬/더) B → B is (much) more than A

보다 marks what you're comparing against — like 'than' in English. Put 보다 after the reference point, and the quality being compared comes at the end. Add 훨씬 (much more) or 더 (more) to make the comparison stronger.

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

일반보다 훨씬 더 할인돼요.
It gets discounted much more than regular (price).

규칙

한국어가 영어보다 어려워요. = Korean is harder than English.
여름이 겨울보다 더워요. = Summer is hotter than winter.
더 = more · 훨씬 = much more, by far
일반보다 더 싸요 = cheaper than regular
일반보다 훨씬 더 싸요 = MUCH cheaper than regular
어느 게 더 싸요? = Which one is cheaper?
한국이 캐나다보다 여름이 더 더워요? = Is Korea's summer hotter than Canada's?

예문

  • 지하철이 버스보다 빨라요.The subway is faster than the bus.
  • 이 회원권이 일반보다 훨씬 더 싸요.This membership is much cheaper than regular.
  • 오늘이 어제보다 더 추워요.Today is colder than yesterday.
