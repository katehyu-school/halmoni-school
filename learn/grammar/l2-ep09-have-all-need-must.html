-아/어야 돼 · -(으)면 돼 · -(으)면 안 돼Have to · That's all you need to · Must not

Level 2핵심 문법🧭 여가·캠핑 · Leisure & Camping
Verb + -아/어야 돼 (must) · Verb + -(으)면 돼 (that's enough) · Verb + -(으)면 안 돼 (must not)

These three patterns all use 돼 (된다) and form a set: one for obligation, one for permission/sufficiency, and one for prohibition. ep09 uses all three!

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

광속으로 예약 버튼을 눌러야 돼. 꾸물거리면 안 돼.
You have to press the booking button at lightning speed. You can't dawdle.

규칙

ㅏ/ㅗ stem + -아야 돼
가다 → 가야 돼 (have to go)

Other vowels + -어야 돼
누르다 → 눌러야 돼 (have to press)
먹다 → 먹어야 돼 (have to eat)

하다 → 해야 돼 (have to do)
9시에 버튼을 누르면 돼. ✅
(All you need to do is press the button at 9.)

이름만 쓰면 돼. ✅
(Just write your name.)
꾸물거리면 안 돼. ❌
(You must not dawdle.)

늦게 일어나면 안 돼. ❌
(You can't wake up late.)
🟢 -(으)면 돼 = OK / sufficient
🔴 -(으)면 안 돼 = NOT OK / prohibited
🔵 -아야 돼 = required / obligatory

예문

  • 숙제를 해야 돼.I have to do my homework.
  • 여기에 이름을 쓰면 돼.All you need to do is write your name here.
  • 수업 중에 핸드폰을 쓰면 안 돼.You can't use your phone during class.
