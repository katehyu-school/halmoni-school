-거든요Background explainer — 'you see' / 'the thing is'

Level 2핵심 문법🌱 환경·변화 · Environment & Change
Verb/Adj stem + -거든(요) · N + 이거든(요) [consonant ending] / 거든(요) [vowel ending]

Use -거든요 to give background information or explain the reason for what you just said. It comes AFTER the main statement — like saying 'you see' or 'the thing is' at the end.

이야기 속에서는 이렇게 쓰입니다

여기가 옛날엔 바다였거든. / 홀딱 반했거든.
This used to be the sea, you see. / We completely fell for it, you see.

규칙

Statement first, then -거든요 · 순서Statement → 거든요 (explanation follows)
'왜 우산 가져가?' → '비가 오거든.' (It's raining, you see.)
'왜 안 먹어?' → '배가 불거든.' (I'm full, that's why.)
Formal vs informal · 거든 vs 거든요-거든 = informal (friends, family, kids)
-거든요 = polite (teachers, elders, strangers)
Ep11: 할머니 uses 거든 with grandkids
In adult conversation: 거든요
-거든요 vs -니까 · 핵심 차이-니까 = reason BEFORE the result
비가 오니까 우산 가져가. (Because it's raining, take an umbrella.)
-거든요 = background AFTER the statement
우산 가져가. 비가 오거든. (Take an umbrella. It's raining, you see.)

예문

  • 나 늦을 것 같아. 차가 막히거든.I might be late. Traffic is bad, you see.
  • 한국 음식 좋아해요. 맛있거든요.I like Korean food. It's delicious, you see.
  • 그 영화 안 봤어. 무섭거든.I haven't seen that movie. It's scary, you see.
