_의 — 소유격 조사 (Possessive Particle)like 's in English · of / 's

Level 1핵심 문법🏯 문화·역사 · Culture & History

N + 의 + N = N's N

의 shows ownership or relationship between two nouns — like 's in English or 'of'. In casual speech, 의 is often pronounced as '에' and sometimes the whole thing is shortened.

규칙

이순신 장군님의 동상 · Admiral Yi's statue
나의 → 내 · 저의 → 제

예문

  • 이순신 장군님의 동상Admiral Yi Sun-sin's statue
  • 조선시대의 첫 궁궐the first palace of the Joseon Dynasty
  • 나의 꿈 → 내 꿈my dream (formal → casual)
💡 팁나의 → 내 / 저의 → 제 are the contracted forms you'll hear most in daily conversation!
